# Puppet Rig

A small, simple tool for making jointed cut-out puppets from your own images (lino prints, drawings, paper cut-outs) and animating them, a bit like a wayang kulit puppet: a body, with limbs that turn at their joints. Somewhere between the physical and the digital world! 

**Use it:** https://hclovecraft00.github.io/puppet-rig/

## What it does

- **Cut:** open an image and click around each piece to cut it out, or upload pieces you printed separately. Paper is removed automatically and the ink texture is kept (*Ink only* for prints, *Remove outside paper* for line art).
- **Rig:** join pieces at their joints (click a piece's joint, then where it attaches). Set which pieces sit in front.
- **Move:** pose by pose like stop-motion (with onion skin), or record live by dragging. Record over a take to add another limb. Shift-click to turn several pieces together; *Mirror* turns them opposite ways.
- **Stamps:** combine several prints into one piece and switch between them frame by frame, so a flipbook of stamps can move.
- **In-betweens:** fill frames in between your own, with easing and arcs, so motion is smoother without making more prints. Timing can follow a BPM.
- **Smoothing:** joint caps, ragged cut edges, and filling where pieces overlapped, all made from your own pixels (nothing AI-generated).
- **Export** GIF or MP4. **Save** a puppet as a file you can open again.

Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Notes

- Built for a laptop with a mouse or trackpad.
- MP4 export needs a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari. GIF works everywhere.
- Undo with Cmd/Ctrl+Z.
